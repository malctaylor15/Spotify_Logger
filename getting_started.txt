After cloning the repository, follow the current "Local Spotify setup" (or
"Remote / headless setup" on a server with no local browser) section in
README.md.

1. Copy `.env.sample` to `.env` and fill in the Spotify client ID/secret and redirect URI.
2. Run `.venv/bin/python config_helper.py` (add `--remote` on a headless server) to save the credentials to `.env` and complete OAuth.
3. Run `bash run_notebook.sh` to create or update `data/listening_history.db`.
4. Optionally schedule `run_notebook.sh` every two hours for continuous collection.

The remaining notes below describe the optional legacy email-reporting and AWS-backup setup. They are not required for Spotify history ingestion.
